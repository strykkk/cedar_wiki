Kemmer comes from a family of gunsmiths, a family tradition of gun making. She is not a knight but a commoner, belonging to the servant class in the Republican Guard. She has gained respect due to the enlightened style of the Jäger Regiment and has achieved a status almost equal to that of the knights, but she is not yet fully accustomed to the Jäger Regiment' unrestrained atmosphere.

Kemmer was extremely interested in firearms and ammunition, and very eager to learn, striving to master any gun-related skills. Unlike the 1st republican Guard, who had priority in ordering supplies, the Jaeger Regiment often faced logistical shortages, and it was difficult to get repairs from manufacturers when their weapons were damaged. This was when Kermer's prowess became crucial; her hand-loaded sniper ammunition was a guarantee of the Jaeger Regiment's accuracy. Kermer had a somewhat naive and timid demeanor, appearing hesitant, but inwardly she was very stubborn. While she was willing to listen to dissenting opinions, she found it difficult to change her initial ideas, and all improvement plans remained essentially the same. To help her comrades, she created a prop gun that fired non-lethal ammunition with beneficial effects—though it still hurt when it hit.
