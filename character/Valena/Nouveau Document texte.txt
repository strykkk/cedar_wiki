Valena Edmondovina was one of the first members of the Eastern Project preparatory team to be sent to Knox. Lacking resources and manpower, and unfamiliar with the land in the United Territory, Valena faced numerous difficulties, but she was undeterred—for her, the search for the "Heritage" held extraordinary significance.

Valena was born in Petrograd (now Ingmarlandia) in 1976. Her father was a nuclear submarine officer, her mother a high school teacher, and her grandfather had served in the Great Patriotic War. She excelled academically from a young age, graduating from the Experimental High School attached to Provitalinsk University, and then entered the Primorsky Combined Operations Command Academy named after Marshal Rokossovsky.

In 1995, Valena graduated and was assigned to the 120th Guards Naval Infantry Division, not far from her hometown. However, the Alliance soon fell into disintegration and civil war. Valena and her companions traveled across almost the entire country and managed to settle in the coastal region thanks to the connections they had built during their military academy days—fortunately, it was not swallowed up by the war, but it became the newly established "Coastal Republic".

However, Valena, having survived the ordeal, did not find peace in the coastal republic for long. The Yugansk Oil Trust, a semi-autonomous behemoth with immense wealth, launched "Project Orient," a search for "heritage" on the planet Knox, seeking assistance from the Coastal Republic. And Knox—that was precisely the place where she had placed her youthful ideals and romantic ideals.
