Benedita is a server at the "Burger Consulate" and the caretaker of its mascot, Wojtek. Possessing a deep empathy for animals and fueled by anger over the rampant abuse and indiscriminate hunting occurring across the Republic, she has become a staunch animal rights advocate.

She devotes most of her time and energy to promoting animal welfare and caring for Wojtek; consequently, while she takes her job seriously, her service is often marked by a stiff smile, mixed-up orders, forgotten dishes, and drinks served without ice. Yet, she never fails to hand out coupons to customers—coupons that feature promotional material for the Animal Protection Association on the back.

Benedita firmly believes that wild animals possess intelligence, even if they lack the means to express it. A living testament to this theory is the burger joint’s mascot, a brown bear named Wojtek. Though massive and slow-moving—sometimes even lumbering—he silently and steadily delivers orders to the right tables, never slacking off or sneaking a bite of the food. Wojtek is so well-behaved that customers often mistake him for a human in a realistic fur costume.

For the owner, Violetta, Wojtek is a major draw for the "Burger Regent," capable of effortlessly hauling crates of frozen meat and gently escorting drunks out of the shop. After all, neither locals nor Earth tourists can resist interacting with a bear that serves food. Standing at the entrance and waving to passersby, Wojtek has joined the delicious burgers and the understanding owner as one of the establishment's biggest attractions. Many customers have jokingly suggested to Violetta that she fire Benedita and hire only Wojtek as a server—though everyone knows full well that neither Wojtek nor Benedita would ever agree to part ways.
