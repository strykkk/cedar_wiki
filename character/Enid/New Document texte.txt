At Sylph's tavern, anyone could tell at a glance that Enid stood out from the crowd: beneath her apron, she wore clothes of exceptional luxury and refinement, and carried herself with elegant, reserved grace. She always treated guests and coworkers with an air of superiority, behaving entirely like a young lady rather than a maid. Yet she was remarkably efficient at her work. Though she was never friendly to guests, she was at least always ready to help, and by professional standards, she seemed beyond reproach.

Enid really did come from the most prominent knightly family in the province, and from a young age had received an education befitting a young lady. The Republic Guard she joined was a personal guard regiment with a ceremonial function, and its members had to provide many splendid gowns and accessories themselves, with her family background, she really ought to have been donating funds to the Republic Guard, not drawing a salary from it.
