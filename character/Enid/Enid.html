Personal History

       At Sylph's tavern, anyone could tell at a glance that Enid stood out from the crowd: beneath her apron, she wore clothes of exceptional luxury and refinement, and carried herself with elegant, reserved grace. She always treated guests and coworkers with an air of superiority, behaving entirely like a young lady rather than a maid. Yet she was remarkably efficient at her work. Though she was never friendly to guests, she was at least always ready to help, and by professional standards, she seemed beyond reproach.

       Enid really did come from the most prominent knightly family in the province, and from a young age had received an education befitting a young lady. The Republic Guard she joined was a personal guard regiment with a ceremonial function, and its members had to provide many splendid gowns and accessories themselves, with her family background, she really ought to have been donating funds to the Republic Guard, not drawing a salary from it.

       Of all the maids, she has the fewest hours on duty and often has to leave town. That's because she has to attend social gatherings for the young ladies of the guard corps. But under Sylph's practical, results-oriented performance management system, Enid's pay is often the highest: in almost every battle, she's the most eager to rack up kills and claim bounties, and she's always the first to lower the bar to meet Sylph's increasingly bizarre demands, though every time she does these things and collects the reward money, she has to say some thing to save face even so...

       Such flexibility made Windsor, the maid captain, who was just as diligent but still somewhat reserved, feel inferior. On the battlefield, the maids noticed that a brass catcher had been fitted over Enid’s Ejection port. She claimed it was because she wouldn’t allow her noble bullets to be picked up by commoners, which left many maid knights of commoner origin feeling resentful. Only the observant maid captain Windsor sensed something was off, Enid seemed to just be saving bullets?

       In reality, Enid's family is no longer prosperous, but to maintain its social status and hide its decline from outsiders, they insist that Enid keep up the airs of a young lady and maintain “normal” social relations with noblewomen. This has left her owing huge sums to tailors and artisans, and she is still paying off the debt in installments, with it growing larger all the time. In the eyes of others, she is the wealthiest in the entire guard unit, but in reality, she is under the greatest financial strain.

Personal Report [TL : Yanqing (延庆)]

       «In Sylph's pub, anyone could tell at a glance that Enid was like a crane standing in a flock of chickens. The clothes underneath her apron were exceptionally gorgeous and exquisite, her temperament and manner of speech were elegant yet reserved. She usually treats the customers and her colleagues with a condescending attitude, much like a rich pampered girl rather than a maid. However, she is exceptionally efficient and skillful at her work, and although she was never very friendly to the customers, she has always been responsive to their requests. Professionally, she's practically impeccable.

       Enid was born into one of the Republic's most prominent lineage of knights, raised like a noble from young. She then joined the Escort Guards of the Sobilin Colour Party, a place with nature like the Guard of Honour, one needs to personally prepare all sorts of elegant ceremonial attires and accessories in there. Her work hours are the least among the maids, always leaving her post to attend gatherings with other distinguished young ladies.

       Logically speaking, from her background, she should have the financial means to fund and support a Guard Regiment, and not have to work yet another job on top of her troop provisions. Unfortunately the young lady's life isn't as glamorous as it seems, In order to maintain her social status and the face needed for «normal» social interactions, she accumulated a considerable debt with tailors and artisans, until now she is still paying off her mortgage.

       No wonder Enid's salary is usually the highest under Sylph's management system that knows nothing other than pragmaticality and results. In almost every battle, she's usually the one who happily kills a few more to collect their bounties, she's also usually the one who was willing to lower her bottom line just to meet Sylph's increasingly weird demands. Such adaptability even made the hardworking and somewhat reserved Head Maid Windsor ashamed of herself for falling behind. Perhaps the Petrusewicz Family which has held the position of the Capital's High Commander throughout generations indeed has such extraordinary and outstanding upbringings that Enid is able to excel in every aspect.»
