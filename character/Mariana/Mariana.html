Personal History

       According to some media reports, Marianne is currently the world's richest person. She is the founder of the private aerospace company Tharsis X, and also a well-known entrepreneur and inventor in e-commerce, social media, consumer electronics, and new energy vehicles.

       Marianne's rise to prominence was much like that of other internet giants, but after earning her initial fortune, she focused on using new technologies to transform traditional industries. In 1997, she actively promoted the West Coast vacuum tube high-speed maglev train project and the "Lighthouse" fully immersive virtual world. After the dot-com bubble burst, she became one of the most active participants in the Vespuccian Manufacturing Revitalization Movement, establishing a close working relationship with President William Heywood.

       However, Mariana's greatest achievement lies in the commercial space sector. Since 1997, Tharsis X has replaced traditional suppliers to become the most important domestic launch partner for the International Space Agency and the Vespuccian military.

       Following the impeachment of President Neil Heywood in early 2000, she shifted her focus again, announcing her intention to revitalize the space exploration business, stating that she would rather end her eventful life on Nox than die in the intensive care unit.
