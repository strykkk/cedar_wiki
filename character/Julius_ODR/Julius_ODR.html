Personal Briefing

       Julius was a typical example of an outstanding officer in Osterheim: conscientious, responsible, professional, and disciplined yet never blindly following orders. Her comrades had long been accustomed to deliberately ignoring the conspicuous animal ears on her head, treating her as an ordinary comrade, and assuming that she possessed a certain primal insight and wilderness skills, unrelated to her pure Nox bloodline.

       Raised by her grandparents, the Royal Forest Hunters of Sobilin Province, Julius developed the ability to traverse various complex terrains from a young age. She excels at gathering information using only simple equipment and her own observation skills, without relying on vehicles or advanced reconnaissance equipment.

       As an officer, Julius's biggest flaw was probably her solitary work style. She preferred to solve problems on her own rather than assign tasks. It was common for soldiers under her command to be unable to find their commander. And Julius never showed any interest in her subordinates' idle chatter unrelated to their duties, never participating in it.

       Julius's military career had been preparing her for a possible war in Europe. She was intimately familiar with the mission she was assigned and was fully confident that she would fulfill it. However, just before the war began, her division headquarters suddenly and mysteriously conveyed an "important mission," requiring her to go behind enemy lines and meet up with an SFV reconnaissance officer to carry out a top-secret special mission.

       Julius calmly accepted the order as usual, but she secretly harbored a rare grumbling in her heart, hoping that this special mission was really important enough to be worthwhile for her to leave behind her comrades in the reconnaissance company and her role as the eyes and ears of the entire division.
