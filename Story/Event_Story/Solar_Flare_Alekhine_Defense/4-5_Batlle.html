Four helicopters flew in a line toward the target. Many soldiers in the Vespuccian army formation, anxious about the impending engagement, naturally assumed upon seeing the gunships that they were returning after just completing a bombing run across the border.
Seeing the helicopters return with sleek, unblemished fuselages, showing no signs of having been hit, the soldiers breathed a collective sigh of relief, surmising that the enemy was likely too weak to put up a fight.
So, people waved at them one after another. However, some also noticed that these strange, cockpit-less helicopters were heavily armed with munitions still slung underneath.
Just as these individuals were filled with suspicion, the unmanned helicopters launched dozens of Hellfire missiles, striking the Vespuccian Army’s 13th Armored Cavalry Regiment as it advanced; caught completely off guard and unable to mount any countermeasure, the unit was left at the mercy of the incoming fire. The Hellfire missiles turned the column into a blazing inferno.
The drone attacks were executed with methodical precision. The unsuspecting Vespuccian military column fell right into the trap. They proceeded to systematically hunt down the enemy convoy, which had not yet deployed for combat. Their tactics were simple: prioritize tanks, then infantry fighting vehicles, and finally, the remaining vehicles.
The stunned enemy attempted to scatter, but the unmanned helicopter immediately adopted a new tactic, targeting vehicles trying to find alternative escape routes and destroying them in the middle of the road, thereby blocking the convoy behind.
This strategy proved highly effective, completely preventing the enemy from dispersing. The helicopters constantly repositioned to target the most vulnerable points of the enemy's armor and engaged light vehicles with autocannons rather than missiles.
Recovering from their initial disarray, and while communications remained intact, the Vespuccian forces began mobilizing their anti-aircraft fire in an attempt to counterattack.
However, the unmanned helicopters immediately responded to the new enemy situation, prioritizing attacks on anti-aircraft vehicles and infantry units carrying anti-aircraft missiles. Even the team members were astonished by the effectiveness of these weapons. They were emotionless, methodically killing people.
The unmanned helicopters had expended their ammunition but remained on station, hovering to assess the results of the strike, as a standard procedure for their mission profile. The surviving ground vehicles finally glimpsed a glimmer of hope and began trying to push aside the wreckage to find an escape route; however, several OTR-21 Tochka missiles rained down from the sky, completely saturating the enemy positions and delivering the final blow.
