Sylvia Oh this is horrible! ...The cat snatched away a doll!

Stray Cat (Hannah cat form trust) Mieooww~~

Prapor …Looks like it doesn’t want to give it up.
Sylvia Mmm, I think I have a solution!
She sewed a few quick stitches, turning the leftover scraps of fabric into a little triangular mouse.
Prapor crouched down and held out the mouse to the cat. The cat’s gaze immediately fixed on its new prey.
Prapor tossed the mouse onto the bench beside him; the cat leapt towards her new toy, leaving the drool-soaked doll on Sylvia’s lap.
