Characters

Karula
Lida

Summary
While exploring the streets, Karula was thrilled to stumble upon a retro anime shop. The store's owner, Ishtar, in a full magical girl cosplay—welcomes the group with open arms. However, the absolute gulf in pricing between local domestic goods from the United Territories and premium imports from Earth leaves Karula completely floored. Meanwhile, Erika, who was trying to buy a secondhand cartridge, faced discrimination and disdain simply because she’s a Nox local...

[On the street of the Cultural and Educational District in the United Territories.]
Elena Well, it's about time for me to report back to the Security Bureau. What time is your meeting scheduled with the EGO Research Institute?

Elena Once we finish our business at hand, let's meet up in front of the management committee building as planned.

Elena Not necessary.
Lisa Then at least leave someone to protect...
