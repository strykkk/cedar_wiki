Summary
The Warrant Officer and his party arrived in Unity City. Due to the dissolution of the Union and the end of the space race, this place has fallen into stagnation and hardship. The only entity interested in continuing to invest in and develop Planet Nox is the private high-tech corporate giant, Marianne. The Warrant Officer recalled the mission he had received on Earth: to search for the Union's mysterious scientific research project on Planet Nox, "The Heritage". He originally had no interest in this, but Valena had accepted the mission alone behind his back and came to Nox...
