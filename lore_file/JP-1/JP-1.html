[] JP INTRODUCTION
- World Setting Introduction
Welcome to the year 2000. Fifty years after humanity ventured into space, we now embark on a journey to an alien planet inhabited by people with animal ears.

Earth was in a slumber of its golden age. Yet, for those who continued to gaze upon the night sky, Earth's companion Planet, Nox, shone brightly.

29 years ago, humanity successfully landed on Nox.

The inhabitants of this planet were people with animal ears who wielded the mystical power "EGO." The "Union" and the "Entente", the two major powers on Earth, competed with each other, dragging this planet, which previously only had 17th-century technology, into the modern era.

Unable to withstand the pressure of the space race, the "Union" disbanded. A civil war broke out between the successor forces, and the other "Entente?" was plunged into chaos. In Knox, where supplies were cut off, the burgeoning industry began to falter.

Now, some Earthlings are once again placing their last hope in Nox and EGO, will the mysterious force "EGO" bring salvation?

And you.

A young veteran soldier who survived the civil war.

The mission is to find the legendary "legacy", a legacy left behind by EGO research during the Union era, a power capable of shaking the heavens and the earth. The fate of these two Planets is up to you.
